# BeamNG cars in the workshop

BeamNG's own cars use their **real factory parts** in the BJR:ME workshop: compatible engines,
gearboxes and drivetrains, suspension and brakes, wheels and tyres, and wings and spoilers. Nothing
is invented: a part is offered only where BeamNG itself says it fits that car, and no new engine
curves, tyre values or brake multipliers are substituted.

## Using it
Open **Motorsport Expansion → Upgrades**, choose a category and a part, then **Apply upgrades**.
**Undo last change** restores the previous parts and settings; **Save setup** keeps the
configuration. A slot with only one choice isn't turned into a fake upgrade ladder, optional parts
(like wings) can be removed, and core parts can't.

## Race rules
Factory parts follow the upgrade caps in **Motorsport settings → Race rules → Upgrades**:
- a factory **engine swap** counts as an engine conversion;
- a factory **turbo or supercharger** counts as forced induction;
- factory **gearbox, suspension and tyre** parts follow their "Factory only" rows;
- factory **wings** follow the aero rule.

Factory engine parts have no Street / Sport / Race level. Every fitted part feeds the car's PI, like
any other upgrade.

## Not yet available for BeamNG cars
Generated BJR:ME upgrades (tyre compounds and widths, weight-reduction stages, aero kits, brake and
engine stages) are only for Automation cars for now. Versions for BeamNG's own cars are being tested
and come in a later update; see the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).
