# Governance

## Maintainer
BJR:ME is maintained by **Sillyw0rld**, its original author. The maintainer sets the project's
direction, reviews contributions and decides what is merged and released.

## How changes are accepted
- Changes to the release branch go through a pull request and review; the branch is protected.
- **Every merge requires the maintainer's approval**, or, once area maintainers are appointed (see
  below), the approval of the area maintainer for that part of the project.
- Tests must pass (`python3 -I tests/run.py`), and performance or rating changes must meet the
  evidence rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Releases and version numbers are made by the maintainer only.

## Decisions
Proposals are discussed in issues. Changes that affect how cars are rated, how races are run or what
players see are decided by the maintainer after discussion, based on evidence where it applies.

## If the project grows
If BJR:ME grows and many people contribute, the maintainer will appoint **area maintainers**:
experienced contributors responsible for one part of the project, for example ratings and PI, the
upgrade workshop and conversions, racing and race control, the living track, the interface, or
installation and releases. Within their area they:
- review pull requests and merge the ones that fit the project's plan and goals;
- help contributors get changes ready, including the evidence ratings and performance changes need;
- review suggestions for new features and bring forward the ones that improve the experience for
  everyone.

The maintainer keeps the final say on the project's direction, on changes that span several areas,
and on releases, and can appoint or replace area maintainers.

## Forks
The licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) allows forks. Forks must keep the copyright notices and NOTICE file,
publish their source to players they serve, and use a different name than "BeamJoy-Revived:
Motorsport Expansion" / "BJR:ME". Improvements are welcome upstream through pull requests.
